## Step 1 (from 2026-11-24): 2 to 4 players, one phone
The work now. Why: `docs/fun-brief.md`. Rules, approved: `docs/step1-rules.md` (build exactly what it says; ask Ben about any gap). Build order, limits and gotchas: `docs/step1-build.md`.
- Built: the rules, `game/` (no UI; `rules.gd` static functions over `state.gd`, every number in `config.gd`, the Sin cards' words in `sins.gd`), tested by `tests/game_test.gd`. A turn is `roll` (Lust then `use` whisper or `settle`), then `buy`, `gift` and `use(key, choices)` as allowed, then `pass_turn`; `block(key, choices)` gives the reason an ability cannot be used. All 14 Tier 1 and Tier 2 abilities are in; the readings the cards do not spell out are listed at the top of `rules.gd`.
- Built: the pass-and-play screen, `ui/table_screen.gd` (flat blockout; `ui/main.tscn` opens it): the How to play card (`HOWTO`), a Sin card behind each Sin's name, the ability buttons, and one line saying who did what to whom. The Match log button (top of the table, and on the winner screen) lists every move newest first, and Copy log puts the match on the clipboard as plain text with each player's ability totals (`ui/match_log.gd`; display only, in 0.21.0). The rows also show LEADING, a brighter box from 850 points, an ability's share of a score ("scored 110 (Hubris +50)") and TIER 1 USED / TIER 2 USED on the turn player. The players' boxes have two sizes (`ROOMY`, `_measure`, `_fit`): roomy, set once a match from the screen's height and the number of players, and compact, the 1920 layout, while a view would not fit with roomy ones (in 0.21.0). Driven as taps by `tests/table_test.gd`. Screenshots: `godot --path . --resolution 1080x2340 --script res://tools/table_shots.gd` -> `build/table_shots/`.
- Ben's rulings since the rules doc was approved are in `docs/step1-rules.md`: no win while in Purgatory; 6 spare dice at any table size; each ability at most once a turn; at most one die bought for yourself a turn (a die bought for another player is extra); first to 1000 (not 3000), Cut a Deal costs 70 and a won Hubris scores the roll plus 50 instead of double (2026-11-26, in 0.19.0), all starting values under test; a Whisper of Desire swap is refused if it is what takes the player to 1000 or more (2026-11-26, in 0.20.0), and so is a Whisper copied by Jealous Grasp (Ben confirmed after 0.20.0 was built; already how 0.20.0 plays); Green-Eyed Glance's copied die replaces one of Envy's dice, which is not rolled, and Glance is not used on two turns running (2026-11-26, in 0.21.0; sim `build/sim_step1/table_sim_glance.gd`, `gopt=preA`).
- 2, 3 or 4 players (Ben, 2026-11-26), chosen on the How to play card; `cfg.seats` in the rules. At two players "twice running" means two of your turns running and Entangling Allure is between Lust and the other player (`docs/step1-rules.md`, "Two players"). Screenshots of that table: add `-- seats=2 dir=table_shots_2p`. In 0.20.0.

## Commands (godot = the Godot 4.7.2 console exe; `tests/run_all.sh` has the path)
