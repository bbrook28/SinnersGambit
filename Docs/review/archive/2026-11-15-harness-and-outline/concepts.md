# Table concepts: my decision, 4 seats

`concepts.png`, left to right: A, B, C. Full size: `assets/art/concepts/table_concept_{a,b,c}.png` (1152x2048). SpriteCook UI-kit concepts, one each, 22 credits each (66 total), style reference: the candlelit table background.

## A: the round gaming table
Rivals sit across the table in arched windows (7); the table's brass rim is the score track, from 0 to a crown at 1000, with one marker per Sin (2, 1). This turn's points are a coin heap in the middle, ready to slide to my edge or be swept (3). Three large candles stand on my edge, one snuffed; Throw has an ember ring (4). Scoring dice glow gold, the Strike is red and smoking, the dead die is grey (5). Bank and Throw sit under the thumbs (6).
Where it departs from the brief: it added a health bar, a coin count and settings, book and banner icons at the top, plus a score plate under each window. That breaks 6 and 8 and doubles the standing readout. The rim's numerals repeat and run into each other ("500 750", "750 30"). My own Sin isn't on screen.
**Weakest point:** the track wraps round a curved rim, so on a phone, distance along the arc is hard to judge at a glance.

## B: the chapel windows and the altar
Four lancet windows across the top, mine lit and the others dim (7). A straight ribbon beneath them runs from 0 to a gate at 1000, each Sin's seal sitting under its own window (2, 1). Below that: dice on an altar cloth (5), a coin heap at stake (3), three candles on the altar's front edge, one snuffed (4), and Bank and Throw at the foot, Throw glowing (6). No sentences; the only numerals are 0, 1000 and the two seal counts (8).
Where it departs from the brief: Throw's "risky" glow is an ember ring, not yet a safe, risky or deadly scale. The markers on the ribbon don't line up with the windows above them.
**Weakest point:** seven windows won't fit in one row at 7 seats. That table needs either two rows or narrower windows, and each portrait gets small.

## C: the descent track
A tall wooden track up the left edge from 0 to a crown at 1000, with a carved pawn per Sin (2). Rivals are in round medallions at the top right, and my Sin is larger and lit at the bottom left (7). The dice are in a felt tray (5), the stake is a coin stack with one numeral (3), the candles sit in a row above the tray (4), and Bank and Throw are under the thumbs (6).
Where it departs from the brief: I asked for my pawn second-highest; it's lowest. The candles sit at the top of the play area, not on my edge. One track label repeats ("500" twice). The stake reads as a small stack beside the tray, not a pile in the middle.
**Weakest point:** the track takes a fifth of the screen's width all game, which squeezes the dice tray and the rivals at 7 seats.
