## Code
- `ui/table_blockout.gd` draws the table whole from one state (`build()`): seats, active seat, dice, stake, Strikes, moment (decision / rival / bust / wait), spoken line, ability die, pending Bank loss, safe insets; under V5 my seal, a power firing, the dice as "let it stand", the Master's line. The table plays `Rules.V5` (`ui/main.gd` `table_rules()`); the old board stays V4. Fixtures (`tools/fixtures/table_*.json`, screen "table") draw one moment.
- `ui/table_play.gd` is a layer over main's board (built in `ui/main.gd` `_build_board`): it redraws the blockout from main's snapshots and turns its seals into main's answers. The game, director, prompts, offer screen, overlays and game end stay main's; the table hides while main shows one of them.
- On only when `Tuning.table_view()`: const `TABLE_VIEW` (false), export feature `table_view` (presets "iOS (table)" and "Web (table)"), or `-- --table`. Test: `tests/table_play_check.gd -- --table` (full V5 games through the table, using the power).

## Layout (1080 px wide; top is them, bottom is me)
- **Windows** across the top, `SIDE` 40 px margins, `WIN_GAP` 12, crop ratio 1:1.8; the active one twice the width, lit in its colour, grown into spare wall above; the others dark.
- **A rival's turn** (REPLY 22, A2): the rival plays on my page. Its stake stands where mine does, in its colour, with "<Sin>'s turn" (44 px) over it; its Strikes snuff my candles; its seal sits in my seal's slot as a flat disc, never live (blazing as it fires), and my seal is hidden. Bank, Throw and my marks dim.
- **Track** the full page width, 0 to 1000. Markers 60 px (`MARK`), display only: the Sin's colour (muted) with its sigil in ink. Rivals above the line on stems (shortest 24 px, levels 70 px apart; close markers fan sideways on leaning stems, two levels, never overlapping); mine below it with a crimson ring and stem; the leader wears a gold ring and crown (none on a tie). The whole track is one tap target: the standings (every seat's score, marks and pending costs, plus the pause menu).
- **Points strip** 110 px between track and dice, always reserved: a throw's points as "+N", Cinzel 80 in deep gold (`GAIN_GOLD`), shown on landing until the next beat adds them to the stake.
- **Dice** one row up to 186 px (`DIE_MAX`), two rows at 140, never under 110 (`DIE_MIN`), 18 px apart; the dice shrink before the windows do. Under each: gold if it scores, crimson if a Strike, nothing if dead. A dashed crimson ghost die for each die a pending cost will take.
- **Stake** one gold numeral above Bank, 120 to 190 px as it grows (to 600); a pending Bank loss in crimson beside it; a bust blots it with spilled ink.
- **Near edge** (`EDGE` 76 px in): Bank (left) and Throw (right) seals in the 236 px (86 pt) box: painted wax (a key, dice; REPLY 26), 178 px at the box's top, no word on it, the word under it in blackletter 48; pressed darkens the wax. My three Strike candles between them (84x300 px), painted: lit with a glow that shrinks with the Strikes taken, or snuffed, darkened with no glow; my marks as sigil stamps (52 px) along the edge.
- **My seal** (`Rules.V5` power, `POWER_SEAL` 180 px, 66 pt), the third control: centred over Throw, 16 px above it. Ready: a glow and ring of my glass colour, bone disc, my sigil in ink, live. Unspent: bone disc, not live. Spent this turn: dark, sigil faint, a crimson stroke. Lust's seal shows the face it would give (a 70 px die).
- **The Master** (REPLY 16-17; painted, REPLY 27): he stands behind the window row over the longest run of rival windows, away from a mark's or offer's target. He is the painted fiddle piece (cb2feec5) as a bust: his hood at mid-skull spans the hood box (two rival windows wide), the crown at its top; masked to crown, hood, skull and shoulders, so no hand, bow or fiddle shows. The wall around him is black. He has no arm: poses rest, speak, call, mark (a gold ring on the leader's window) and offer (a card centred just above the offering Sin's window, inside its width) are the plate alone. He is drawn where his boxes say at every size (his breath scales about his foot, and his position takes that pivot back out; REPLY 31). His line sits on the painted plate (8de13e46, a nine-patch), its top 8 px under his jaw, clear of the active window and of the card in his hand (REPLY 17); its ends follow his outline at a two-line plate's middle, his own line never narrower than 380 px (REPLY 23), and widened to one line when the room clear of the active window and the card allows it (REPLY 32). The raised hand stays at least 36% of an arm's width (60 px at most) below the safe line (REPLY 17). Windows: build 116's size (the row took the old gap; 22 px left for my crimson bar). Inactive windows are lifted to 0.3, with an ink frame. Every choice row but the game end (buying, an ability's yes or no, a target) sits on its own plate on the page under the track: the question (wrapping to two lines), then buttons 128 px tall, four a line (buying: all on one), a name or "Buy N" over its number; `build()` keeps the plate's room, so the dice give way (REPLY 23). Nothing sits on the window band. The track band's pads are 14 px. Pixel boxes: `tools/boxes.gd`. Offer beat: before an offer's card screen, he holds the card up on the table for `MASTER_OFFER_BEAT` 1.2 s; a tap skips.
- **Page** lit in a pool round the dice and decision, falling to shadow; the desk is dark.

## Behaviour
- Motion is simple: a throw's dice drop in, the stake swells when it grows, a Strike flashes its candle, a bust shakes the page.
- A spoken line (`Tuning.LINE_CHANCE` 0.5 of events) shows in blackletter 60 px in the speaker's colour at the foot of the speaker's own window (approved exception 2).
- Choices other than Bank and Throw (an ability's yes or no, a target, buying dice) are flat bone buttons in the gap under the windows with the prompt's words above; the buy prompt reads just "Buy dice?".
- Powers (`Rules.V5`): when my power could change the throw just made, the beat holds with no time limit: tap my seal to use it, or the dice to let the throw stand; no prompt sentence. Pride names a Bank (150, 200, 250) on bone buttons in the gap, or just throws. Greed's seal sits beside Bank and Throw at the decision. A power firing (mine or a rival's, at its window) holds `Tuning.POWER_FOR` 1.1 s: a ring of that Sin's colour and its sigil on each die it changed, or round the stake it took (a rival's sigil beside its Strike marks).
- Every line (a Sin's or the Master's) holds at least `Tuning.LINE_MIN` 3.5 s at 1x, bots' turns too; a line arriving meanwhile is dropped, except his call, final round, mark and end, which wait (REPLY 22). Besides those, the Master says at most one line a round (the idle line included). Said while the table is hidden (an offer), his line waits for the table. His idle line after `MASTER_IDLE` 9 s at my decision. At an offer his announcement sits above the portrait.
- Not built on the table: hold to explain (still a rule, `CLAUDE.md`). Painted: page, table top, seals, candles, the Master, the line plate. The jester is cut (REPLY 1). The skeleton is gone (REPLY 14); the Master is a flat placeholder.
- No tutorial in the table build (`ui/main.gd` skips it when `table_view()` is on).

## Offer screen (main's, shown over the table)
The board dims fully (`OFFER_DIM` 1.0). The Sin's 4:5 portrait spans 88% of the width from the top with a flare, then its name (64), its spoken line inked on in blackletter in its colour (60), the terms on two lines (gain over cost, 50) fading in after the line dries, one context line (38) and, the first time, the tip. Accept and Refuse seals sit 28 px above the safe area; the words end at least 48 px above them. Portraits use the flat image with living-glass light only (`Tuning.LAYERS_PARKED`: all seven Sins).
