# Table design (Phase 7 rework, draft 2, 2026-11-14)
The table game is the real game again: Play opens it. Your opponents are the Seven Sins, and they tempt you to push too far. The run game is parked (`run/`, `ai/run_bot.gd`, `sim/run_sim.gd` and their tests stay, shown only behind `Tuning.RUN_MODE`). Balatro is the bar for polish, not structure.
Base rules stay `Rules.V3`: throw all your dice; 4, 5, 6 score 5x face; several scorers in one throw x1.5 / x2 / x2.5; 1 and 2 are Strikes; three Strikes bust (lose the turn, start the next in Purgatory); Bank keeps the turn; buy dice for 100 after a Bank; first to 1000 wins. All new values go in a new `Rules.V4`, so V3 tests and saves still replay.

## 1. Rule changes (each needs Ben's OK)
1. A throw's points round down to a multiple of 5 (two scorers 20+25 = 67.5 now shows 65).
2. Nothing asks a player who isn't holding the phone: off-turn abilities (Fury, Untouchable) become automatic.
3. Always-yes abilities become automatic: Green-Eyed Glance, Mimicry, Whisper of Desire, Untouchable (§5).
4. No percentages: Interest is a flat 50, Binding Pact takes a flat 50, The Vig is 20 (§5).
5. Envy nerf (win share 2.06 at 4 seats): Mimicry makes Glance add two dice, not three extra in total.
6. "Greater" leaves the screen: the second ability reads "From 300, once a round:". First Game (Basic only) stays for your first game and Circle 1.
7. New: temptations, marks and claims (§7), house rules and perks in Solo (§6).

## 2. Pass-and-play
- Menu: **Solo** (the ladder), **Table** (setup), Continue. Table setup: 2-7 seats, each a Sin portrait (tap for the carousel; no Sin twice) with a Human / Bot toggle, and a Temptations toggle (on). Defaults: 4 seats, you + 3 bots. One human + bots is the quick game. Sins unlock for both modes (§6).
- Handoff beat before a human's turn when the last human turn was someone else's: the next Sin's window fills the screen in its colour, "Pass to Wrath", tap to start. No names: each human is their Sin.
- Temptations come from the game in the offering Sin's voice; a human playing that Sin doesn't make them (the phone is with the tempted player). Only rival Sins tempt you.
- CLAUDE.md v1 line: solo ladder and pass-and-play, one phone; no online play.

## 4. Rivalry
- Each bot keeps a grudge list for the game: whoever aimed an ability at it (Fury, Pact, Drag). Paying one back clears that grudge.
- Targeting (Pact, Drag; Wrath's Fury fires at 100+ on a grudge, 150+ on anyone): the newest grudge, unless the leader is 200+ ahead of that seat; then the leader. The bot says a "Takes aim" line naming the target; an "Attacked" line answers.
- Each Sin's bot plays to its nature: Wrath, Lust and Gluttony throw one more time than a plain bot would; Sloth and Greed bank one throw earlier; Pride and Envy plain. The sim checks this keeps shares fair.
- `ai/` changes: `bot_policy.gd` gains `grudges`, `pick_target()` (replaces `game.leader_other` in Lust and Sloth), a per-Sin `risk` table and `wants_temptation()` (§7). In Solo, a `memory` of past tables (who you beat, which host) feeds the Recall lines. `run_bot.gd` untouched.

## 5. Bot turns and abilities
- A bot turn is one beat, 2.5 s or less at 1x (`Tuning.BOT_TURN_MAX`): window forward 0.25 s, each throw 0.35 s (compressed when there are more), result (Bank number or BUST) 0.5 s, then its line while the window settles. A temptation offered to a bot takes 0.4 s from the throws. No log text, no "thinking".

| Sin | Basic (new wording) | From 300, once a round |
|---|---|---|
| Wrath | **Spark of Rage:** +40 whenever a rival busts. | **Fury:** The first rival throw worth 150+ rerolls its three best dice. (auto) |
| Greed | **The Vig:** When a rival buys a die, you get 20 of its price. | **Interest:** Bank and buy no dice: +50. |
| Gluttony | **Feast:** Once a turn, tap a die to feed it for +50; a fed Strike doesn't count. | **Gorge:** Your first die each turn costs 70. |
| Lust | **Whisper of Desire:** Once a turn, your 3 or 2 becomes the last player's best die. (auto) | **Binding Pact:** Tap a rival; take 50 of the next turn they Bank. |
| Envy | **Green-Eyed Glance:** Your first scoring throw each turn gains a copy of the last player's best die. (auto) | **Mimicry:** That throw gains two copies instead. (auto) |
| Pride | **Hubris:** Before throwing, bet 100, 150 or 200: Bank that much for x1.5, else 0. | **Untouchable:** The first ability aimed at you bounces back. (auto) |
| Sloth | **Lazy Bones:** Your first Strike each turn doesn't count. | **Drag:** Tap a rival; they throw 3 fewer dice next turn (never below 1). |
Choices left: Feast (which die, or none), Hubris (bet or not), Pact and Drag (which rival). Floors as now: Feast and Gorge keep you at 2 dice.

## 6. Solo: the ladder
- Seven Circles in order; each is one table game to 1000: C1-2 you + 2 bots, C3-7 you + 3 bots. One bot is the **host**, whose house rule binds every seat, host included. C1 is a First Game (Basic only); bots grow bolder each Circle. Hosts: the other six Sins in random order (only Sloth, Greed or Envy host C1-2); C7 is hosted by your own Sin. The other bots are drawn from Sins you've met.
- Win to go down a Circle; lose and replay that table (perks and marks stay). Progress saves at every table start. After each win, choose one of three perks; perks last the whole descent.
- Unlocks: Sloth and Wrath to start; beating a host unlocks that Sin for Solo and Table.

| Greed | Each Bank costs 25. | Wrath | A bust also costs 50 of your score. |
| Envy | The leader throws one fewer die. | Pride | A Bank under 100 scores nothing. |
| Gluttony | Everyone starts with 5 dice; dice cost 150. | | |

Perks (15): 1 Start every table with 4 dice. 2 Start every table with 100. 3 Your first die each table is free. 4 Every 6 you throw scores 10 more. 5 Three scorers in one throw: +50. 6 Your first throw each turn has one extra die. 7 Purgatory never costs you. 8 Once a table, a bust keeps its points. 9 +20 whenever a rival busts. 10 Your second ability is ready from the start. 11 Refuse a temptation: +25. 12 Accept a temptation: +25. 13 Bank 200 or more: +25. 14 The first ability aimed at you each table misses. 15 Cleanse: remove every mark of one Sin (once).

**Endings** (after C7, by marks taken over the whole descent): **The Saint** (3 marks or fewer); **The Eighth Sin** (marks from all seven Sins); otherwise **Claimed**: the Sin you took the most marks from gives you its seat (7 endings, one painted scene and 3 lines each). Nine endings; the collection shows which you've seen. Endings and perk icons use placeholder art until Phase 9.

## 7. Temptations, marks, claims
- At a throw-or-bank decision, at most once a turn, on about one turn in three (`Tuning.TEMPT_CHANCE`), never in a game's first round, a First Game or Circle 1: one rival Sin offers. Pick weight 1, +2 for each of its marks the player holds, so a Sin that has you hooked comes back. The offer is a scroll from its window, 12 words or fewer, **Accept** / **Refuse**, one tap. The first offer you ever see comes with one tip: "Accept for the gain and take its mark. Three marks, and it claims you." Bots get offers too; the table sees them accept or refuse with a line.
- Every offer is one gain and one cost. Gains: **points now**; **clear a Strike**; **extra dice this throw** and **x2 this throw** (accepting throws at once); **a free die** (from the Supply). Costs: **lose points now** (never below 0); **lose points from your next Bank**; **start next turn with a Strike**; **fewer dice next turn** (never below 1); **lose a die** (after this turn, never below 2, back to the Supply). A pending cost is a chip on your plate until paid; a bust doesn't pay it.
- Accepting gives you that Sin's **mark** (Sin-coloured pips by your window). At 3 marks of one Sin, its **claim** binds you for the rest of the table and those marks clear. Sim target 0.5-1.5 claims per 4-seat game, tuned by `TEMPT_CHANCE` first, then the threshold. Solo counts every mark ever taken for the endings.

## 8. Terms a new player learns (8)
Strike, Bust, Bank, Purgatory, Temptation, Mark, Claim, Circle. Plain words, not terms: seat, house rule, perk, host (shown as "Wrath's table"). "Greater", "Supply", "Line", "Ladder" never appear on screen.
