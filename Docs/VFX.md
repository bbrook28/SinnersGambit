# VFX brief (Ben, 2026-11-13). For the juice phase, after the look pass.

## Numbers and text
- Score ticks up digit by digit, each tick with a small scale pop and a gold spark.
- Gold-leaf shader on all numbers: slow sheen sweep, plus a glint that follows phone tilt.
- Big scores slam in: overshoot scale, white flash frame, shockwave ring, embers falling off the digits.
- Multiplier burns in like a brand: ember edge, smoke wisp, then settles to gold.
- Points fly from the dice to the turn total, then from the total to your Sin's plate on Bank, as streaks of gold dust.
- Banner text is written on by an invisible quill, ink wet for a moment before drying.

## Sin windows
- Idle: slow light shimmer through the glass, as if a candle moves behind it. Figures breathe and blink.
- Active Sin: window brightens and throws its colour as a light shaft onto the stage.
- Ability trigger: glass flares white-hot, leading cracks, shards burst outward and reassemble.
- Taking a Strike: a crack spreads across that Sin's pane and stays for the round.
- Leader: faint halo of gold motes around the top-scoring window.

## Dice
- Roll: motion blur, bone dust on each bounce, a spark on every table hit, screen shake scaled to dice count.
- Landing: squash, a dust ring on the leather, a shadow that snaps in.
- Scoring dice: pips ignite in gold one by one, then the die lifts and glows.
- Dead dice: sink into shadow and desaturate.
- Held dice: a thin ring of candlelight under them.

## Strikes
- A nail slams in with a hammer flash, splinters and a heavy haptic; red seeps from the hole.
- Third Strike: all three nails glow, the stage light gutters, the turn total burns to ash and blows away.

## Buttons
- Wax seals depress with a soft deform and spring back; a ripple of gloss crosses the wax.
- Roll pulses with a slow ember glow when it's the recommended action.
- Bank: coins burst from the seal and stream to your plate.

## World
- Candle flame flickers and drives all scene lighting; gutters on a Strike, flares on a big score.
- Dust motes drift through the candlelight in front of everything.
- Vignette breathes with the candle.
- Turn change: page-turn wipe with a paper shadow.

## Rules
- Every effect has anticipation, impact and settle; nothing just appears.
- Effects scale with the size of the event; small scores stay small.
- Nothing may hide the dice faces or the score for more than 0.3 s.
- Reduce Motion swaps shake, flashes and parallax for fades.
