# Step 1 rules: 4 players, one phone

Status: **draft for Ben, 2026-11-24. Nothing is built.** Brief: `docs/fun-brief.md`.
Sources: Ben's 2025 printed rulebook (`Sinners Gambit.pdf`, 21 pages), his table rulings of 2026-11-24, and the Sin cards in the Google Doc rulebook. Where the PDF and the Google Doc differ, the PDF wins. The PDF has no Sin cards, so every ability below is the Google Doc's, copied as written: no cost or number is changed.

## The game
- **Goal:** first to 3000 points. It is checked at the end of your own Fate Phase, and you win at once.
- **Players:** 4, passing one phone. No bots.
- **Dice:** 27 in all. Each player starts with 3; the other 15 are the shared supply. No one ever drops below 2.
- **Sins:** dealt at random, one each, shown to everyone.
- **Start:** everyone at 0 points. Turns go round in seat order.

## Your turn
**1. Fate Phase**
- Roll all your dice once. No reroll unless an ability gives one.
- A 4, 5 or 6 scores 40, 50 or 60. A 1, 2 or 3 scores nothing.
- **Purgatory:** if every die shows 1, 2 or 3, you score 0 and your turn ends.
- Otherwise add up your scoring dice and go on to the Gambit Phase.

**2. Gambit Phase**, in any order, as often as you like, then pass:
- **Talk.** Promises are not binding. Lying is allowed.
- **Trade** (the three gifts, below). A trade happens only if both players agree, and then it is permanent.
- **Use abilities:** one Tier 1 and one Tier 2 a turn.
- **Buy dice:** 20 points each from the supply, any number you can pay for. You roll them from your next turn.

## Purgatory
- While you are in Purgatory you cannot buy, trade or use abilities.
- On your next turn, roll your dice as usual. Two or more dice showing 1, 2 or 3 frees you and you play on. Fewer, and your turn ends; you try again next turn.

## The three gifts
- **Give points:** any amount you have, to any player.
- **Lend a die:** one of your dice goes to another player for one roll, then it goes to the shared supply (not back to you). You cannot go below 2 dice.
- **Buy a die for another player:** you pay 20, and the die from the supply is theirs to keep.
- None of these is allowed to or from a player in Purgatory.

## Abilities
Tier 1 is open from the start. Tier 2 opens when you reach 350 points and stays open even if you drop below. Tier 3 is not in step 1.

| Sin | Tier 1 (free) | Tier 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Wrath | **Spark of Rage:** make a player reroll one die showing 5 or 6. If it comes up 1-3 they score 0 for it and lose 10. | **Vengeful Strike (50):** target chooses: lose 50, or give you 1 die for 2 full rounds. Not the same target twice running. |
| Greed | **The Vig:** passive. When an opponent buys a die, 20 of its price goes to you. | **Cut a Deal (50):** target chooses: pay you 50, or give you 1 die for 1 full round. Not the same target twice running. |
| Lust | **Whisper of Desire:** before scoring, swap one of your dice with a higher die of another player's. | **Entangling Allure (40):** choose 2 players; each secretly picks 1 die. Match: you take one. No match: they swap. |
| Envy | **Green-Eyed Glance:** add a temporary die to your roll, matching any die an opponent just rolled. | **Jealous Grasp (30):** copy any Tier 1 ability used this round. Not the same one twice running. |
| Pride | **Hubris:** before you roll, declare. Beat the previous player's total and your score for the roll doubles; fail and it is 0. First player must beat 50. | **Superiority (40):** passive. The first time you are targeted each round, the attacker loses 20. |
| Gluttony | **Feast:** once a round, give one of your scoring dice (4-6) to the supply and gain 150. | **Voracious Bite (20):** steal 20 from a player. Not the same target twice running. |
| Sloth | **Slow Drip:** if you score under 60 this turn, gain 40 more. | **Lethargic Aura (30):** next turn the target rolls 1 die twice and keeps the lower. You gain 10. |

**Lust's Entangling Allure on one phone.** As written it needs two secret picks at the same moment. Smallest change: the phone is passed to each chosen player in turn; each taps one die from their own last roll with the screen showing nothing to the others; then both picks are shown together. "Match" means the two dice show the same number. Nothing else about it changes.

## Not in step 1
Tier 3 abilities, the chain multiplier, bets, the Master's call, comeback offers, temptations, marks, claims, bots, new art.

## Open questions
Only what stops a match from finishing, or what the code cannot be written without. Nothing here is a balance change.

1. **Can a player with 2 dice ever leave Purgatory?** To escape they need both dice to show 1-3, which is also the roll that sends you there. If the Purgatory check applies to the escape roll, a 2-dice player is trapped for the rest of the match. *Suggested:* the escape roll is never checked for Purgatory; once free, you score its 4s, 5s and 6s and play on.
2. **Can points go below 0?** The PDF does not say. *Suggested:* no. A loss stops at 0, and you cannot pay a cost you do not have.
3. **Two winners.** The PDF says you win at once, and also gives a tie-breaker for players who reach 3000 "in the same cycle". *Suggested:* the first player to end a Fate Phase at 3000 or more wins; no tie-breaker in step 1.
4. **Dice on a phone have no faces between rolls.** Lust's Whisper, Envy's Glance and Wrath's Spark all act on another player's dice. *Suggested:* each player's last roll stays on screen until they roll again, and those are the dice these abilities see.
5. **When Wrath's Spark is used.** It changes a roll before it scores, so it cannot wait for Wrath's own Gambit Phase. *Suggested:* after any other player's roll, the phone asks Wrath once whether to use it, at most once a round.
6. **"The previous player's total" for Hubris.** *Suggested:* the points the player before you scored on their last roll (0 if they were in Purgatory).
7. **When Pride pays for Superiority.** It is passive but costs 40. *Suggested:* Pride pays 40 in their own Gambit Phase to arm it until their next turn.
8. **A die taken by an ability.** The PDF says taken dice come back before the owner's next Fate Phase; Wrath's and Greed's cards say 2 rounds and 1 round. *Suggested:* the card's own length wins.
9. **Does the Vig apply when you buy a die for someone else?** *Suggested:* yes, the buyer pays it, as with any die bought.
10. **Who may give during a Gambit Phase?** *Suggested:* the player whose turn it is offers a gift or asks for one; the other player taps to agree. A swap is two gifts.
