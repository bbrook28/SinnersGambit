# Sinners' Gambit 0.14.0: gameplay, onboarding and documentation audit

Date: 2026-11-23. Commit audited: 3801639 (0.14.0). Status: findings only; nothing in the game was changed.

The hand works and is well explained for someone who takes the lessons, but the game's own hint gives wrong advice under Pride. A quarter of hands ask one decision or none, and there is still nothing to play for beyond the hand.

## How it was done
- Read every doc in `docs/superpowers/`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/handoff.md`, and every script in `dice/`, `ui/`, `sim/dice_sim.gd`, `tests/` and `tools/`.
- Ran `bash tests/run_all.sh` (all passed) and `tests/load_check.gd` (passed, 16 scripts and scenes).
- Ran `sim/dice_sim.gd` at 20,000 hands, seed 4, with no Sin and with `demand=pride`.
- Played the three lessons, free hands under Pride and the `?` page in a web build at 390x844, from a copy of the project holding only the folders the game uses.
- Checked suspicions with a throwaway probe script against the exact solver and the screen (not kept in the repo).

## Bugs (each reproduced)
1. **The hint ignores the Sin.** Holding one 6 under Pride, the line under the row says "One more 6: Pair · 1" (`next_hint`, `ui/dice_screen.gd:876`, scores with `Rules.score`, the poker hand alone). The solver puts a second 6 at 4.76 marks against 5.66 for a 3, 4 or 5: following the hint costs 0.91 marks. Best play's first kept die is a 6 in 14.6% of hands. In lesson 3 the hint sits directly under the instruction "Keep the 4". The same blindness makes "One more 2: Pair · 1" and "Needs 2 and 5: Run · 5" silent about the 2's cost.
2. **Replay the tutorial during a roll throws a script error.** The `?` page opens while the dice are still rolling (phase `anim`); "Replay the tutorial" then clears the tray under the running roll: `Out of bounds get index '0'` at `ui/dice_screen.gd:509`, plus three `_flicker` tween errors. The game recovers into lesson 1.
3. **Skipping the lessons teaches no skulls.** The tutorial design says the one-time cards for the first skull and the first Last Dance still appear for a player who skipped. No such cards exist: the code only sets `seen["skull"]` and `seen["dance"]` (`ui/dice_screen.gd:521`, `:534`). A skipper gets the Sin card and one message line at the Last Dance. No test covers this.
4. **A local export is 1.6 GB.** The presets use `export_filter="all_resources"` with `exclude_filter="sim/*, tests/*, tools/*, Docs/*"`, so `build/` (1,503 imported files here) is packed; `Docs/*` no longer matches anything. A copy holding only `assets/art/runtime`, `master`, `ui`, fonts, audio and icons exports at 68 MB. CI has no `build/`, but still ships all of `assets/`, including folders the game never loads (`art/sins`, `art/seals`, `art/concepts`, `art/bg`, `art/dice`, `art/extra`); that size was not measured.

## Gameplay
Best play under Pride, 20,000 hands, seed 4:

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Hands with no real keep choice | 12.3% |
| Hands with one or none | 24.8% |
| Rolls that end with nothing to decide | 43% |
| Keeps between identical dice (two taps, no choice) | 0.46 a hand |
| Rolls a hand | 4.82 |
| Busts | 21.8%, of them 28.5% on the Last Dance |
| Marks a hand | median 6, lower quartile 2, upper quartile 7, top tenth 8 |
| Full rows that pay 0 | 0.9% |

- **The roll-count choice is nearly flat.** On the first roll, 1, 2 or 3 dice are worth 4.88, 4.92 and 4.80 marks under Pride (3.58, 3.49, 3.36 with no Sin). With a pair and one skull the spread is 0.16. It only matters at two skulls, where one die is plainly right (2.41 against 2.29 and 2.05).
- **No stakes.** A bust costs only that hand's marks, the total never falls, and marks buy nothing (the docs already call this a separate gap). Replaying the tutorial pays its scripted 12 marks again each time.
- **The no-Sin sim misses three of its four pass marks** on the shipped config: best play beats greedy by 11% (mark 15%), busts 19.8% (mark 20-30%), Last Dance 38.4% (mark 15-30%). Under Pride the four demand marks pass (0.44 questions a hand, the hand wins 39%, 2.74 choice keeps, Sin 1.95 against hand 3.00) and the Last Dance band is missed again at 37.9%. Either the marks or the game are out of date; the docs do not say which.
- **Two identical dice still need a pick and a Keep.** The rules keep a die for you only when one die is left.

## Onboarding and UI
- **Cards cover what they describe.** "The ribbon above your row…" and "Under each button…" appear over an 84% dim that hides the ribbon and the buttons. The first skull is covered by the "More dice, more risk" card as soon as it lands.
- **Terms appear before they are taught**, which the tutorial design lists as a failure: "No match · 0", the hint line, and the risk captions ("no risk", "under 1% to lose it") all show in lesson 1.
- **Guided steps are weakly signposted.** The wanted button is only a little brighter than the two disabled ones; a tap on the wrong die or button gives no feedback; nothing says "lesson 2 of 3"; the button between lessons reads "Next hand".
- **Small text.** At a 390 pt wide phone (1080 design px): hand notes 9.4 pt, "if kept" 10.1 pt, risk captions 10.5 pt, the ANY label 10.8 pt, hint 12.3 pt, message line 13 pt, card text 13.7 pt. The old project's own floor was 15 pt. The `?` button is 33 pt, under the 44 pt touch guideline.
- **No settings:** sound, haptics, reset progress.
- **Smaller points.**
  - The first tap during a Sin's opening only ends the opening; the button under it is not pressed.
  - "Replay the tutorial" sits under Back at the same weight, asks no confirmation and abandons the hand.
  - The `?` page is titled "The hands" but holds all the rules; the rules scroll in a short window under the fixed ranking.
  - The table-edge rule shows behind the glass during the Sin's opening.
  - Pride's window hangs on the wall through lessons 1 and 2, where no Sin watches.
  - An auto-kept die leaves the mat quickly, so the roll that decided the hand is hard to read.

Evidence: screenshots of the hint against the lesson line, the opening, and the "A Pair" card over the ribbon were sent in the audit session; `build/shots/29_l3_second_6_minus_4.png` and `25_l3_sin_opening.png` show the first two at full size.

## Documentation
- **No current rules document.** `2026-11-20-dice-poker-pitch.md`, which `dice/rules.gd` cites, still says bag 8, cash out and two dice a throw, and is marked "draft for Ben's approval". The rules as built live in `CLAUDE.md` and in `dice/config.gd` comments.
- **`CLAUDE.md` is behind.** It says Pride, Envy and Wrath host (`HOSTS` is Pride alone), calls the demand "0.12.0, a prototype" (0.13.0 changed it to exactly one 6), and calls the lone-six plan a draft (the plan says approved).
- **`.claude/handoff.md` describes 0.11.2** and a pitch written three builds ago.
- **Statuses contradict.** The Sin's demand pitch is "draft for Ben's approval" but shipped in 0.13.0.
- **Stale comments.** `dice/config.gd` says the Last Dance comes in about 25% of hands (38%); `ui/dice_screen.gd:60` says all seven Sins host; `ui/main.gd` and `tests/ui_test.gd` still say "the guided first hand".
- **The tutorial design promises cards that do not exist** (bug 3).
- **CI runs only `tests/load_check.gd`,** not `tests/run_all.sh`.
- **The public repo has no README and no LICENSE** (the font licences are present).
- **Two untracked docs about a gameplay-feel skill** sit in `docs/superpowers/` and are not about this game.

## Not covered
