# The Tutorial (0.14.0) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

Status: **OK from Ben, 2026-11-21; built as 0.14.0.**

**Goal:** Three short scripted lessons that teach every word and number on the screen to someone who has never played poker or dice, with plainer words throughout and a ? page that holds all the rules.

**Architecture:** `ui/dice_tutorial.gd` holds the lessons as data (scripted faces, steps, the Sin watching); `ui/dice_screen.gd` plays them through the hooks it already has (`_on_seal`, `_on_die`, `_on_keep`, `_tut_sync`, `_tut_msg`) and moves to the next lesson on "Next hand". The renames change shown strings only; no rule changes.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2, GDScript. Tests: `bash tests/run_all.sh`. Shots: `tools/shots.gd`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-tutorial-design.md` (approved).

## Global Constraints

- Shown words: roll (never draw or throw), Run (not Straight), Three and a pair (not Full house), No match (not High die), "counts as any number" and the die label ANY (never wild), "exactly one 6" (never lone), "is watching" (never hosts), button (never seal), "puts out a candle" (never snuffs).
- Kept words: skull, marks, the bag, your row, Keep, the Last Dance, "The Master takes the hand".
- Function, variable and config names do not change (`Rules.throw`, `draw_options`, `is_dance`, the order of `Rules.HANDS`).
- No card over 45 words. Never two cards in a row without a roll between them, except at a lesson's start.
- Tests first in every task; commit after each; nothing is pushed until Ben says. Never commit `project.godot`.
- Evidence goes to Ben without a verdict: shots at phone shape and at 4:3.

## Review Focus

1. The app is closed in the middle of a lesson: the next launch starts lesson 1 again and nothing is half-saved. (Task 3)
2. "Replay the tutorial" tapped from the ? page in the middle of a hand Pride watches: lesson 1 starts clean, with no Sin's line and no tags. (Task 3)
3. "Skip the lessons" on lesson 2 or 3: free play starts, and the one-time card for Pride still appears because the player never saw lesson 3. (Task 3)
4. The longer line "Exactly one 6 pays 4. Each 2 costs 1." still ends left of the first candle at 30 px or more, at 1080x2340 and at 1536x2048. (Task 1)
5. A tap on a button or die the lesson has not asked for does nothing: no roll, no keep, no step advanced. (Task 3)
6. "Three and a pair · 5" beside "+ Pride · 4" stays inside the ribbon's paper. (Task 1)
