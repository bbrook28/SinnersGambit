# The lone 6: plan for 0.13.0

Status: **approved by Ben, 2026-11-21, with three changes (the shorter line, the long opening on the first hand only, six hands of play).** Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-sin-demand-pitch.md` (second revision, a draft until Ben approves it).
Goal: one build where Pride hosts every hand with "a 6 that stands alone pays 4, each 2 costs 1", plus the review's screen fixes, for Ben to play six hands.
The hand's rules do not change; the refusal is a separate pitch and is not in this build. Tests first in every task; commit after each; nothing is pushed until Ben says.

## 1. Rules (`dice/`), tests in `tests/dice_test.gd`
- `demands.gd`: a demand gains `"lone": {face: marks}` (paid when the row holds exactly one of that face). Pride becomes `{"lone": {6: 4}, "faces": {2: -1}, "danger": 0, "line": "A lone 6 pays 4. Each 2 costs 1."}`. Envy and Wrath stay in the file, tested, and do not host.
- `rules.gd`:
  - `sin_marks` adds the lone faces.
  - `total(...)` also returns `wild_as`: the face the wild die was paid as (0 when the row has none).
  - `keep_delta(s, i: int) -> int`: what keeping thrown die `i` does to the Sin's pay (`sin_marks` of the row with it, less without).
- Tests (exact values):
  - Row `[6, 3, 3, 4, 5]`: hand 1, Sin 4, total 5. Row `[6, 6, 3, 4, 5]`: hand 1, Sin 0. Row `[6, 2, 2, 4, 5]`: hand 1, Sin 2, total 3.
  - Row `[2, 2, 3, 4, 5]`: hand 1, Sin −2, total 0.
  - Row `[3, 3, 4, 0]` paid on an empty bag: the wild is a 6 (pair 1 + 4 = 5), not a 3 (three of a kind 3); `wild_as` is 6.
  - `keep_delta`: a thrown 6 is +4 with no 6 held, −4 with one held, 0 with two; a thrown 2 is −1.
  - Today's Envy and Wrath rule tests pass unchanged.

## 2. Sim (`sim/dice_sim.gd`, `dice/policies.gd`)
- The sim reports, for strong under a demand: **questions asked a hand** (keeps where no thrown face is best for both the no-Sin game and the Sin-only game) with how often each side wins, and **choice keeps a hand**. The solver gains a Sin-only mode for this (the hand worth nothing).
- Pass marks from the pitch: questions 0.4 a hand or more and neither side over 70%; choice keeps 2.33 or more; the Sin pays less than the hand. The gaps over greedy and the bust rate are printed, not judged.
- Run `demand=pride`, seed 4, 20000 hands, one process. The probe predicts 0.43 questions, 40% / 60%, 2.73 choice keeps, hand 2.98 against Sin 1.94.
- **Stop point:** if the sim disagrees with the probe by more than sampling noise, or a mark misses, I stop and report before any screen work.
